# 90-second demo: shot list

Record this after a real checkpoint exists. A smoke model will not survive the first shot.

1. **0–15 s.** Landing page. One sentence on screen. Click Play. Arrow keys move the paddle. The FPS counter is visible.
2. **15–40 s.** Keep playing long enough that drift shows up, or cut to the moment it does. Do not edit the failure out.
3. **40–65 s.** Lab. Real frame and dream frame, the MAE curve, the step slider moved from 1 to 3, then re-ground. The context snaps back.
4. **65–85 s.** Agent mode. The dream-trained policy plays. Cut to the real-game mean and std from `RESULTS.md`, including the seed count.
5. **85–90 s.** The URL and the repo.

Voiceover should say the model was trained on free GPUs, that v1 is the baseline on the same curve, and the horizon the curve actually reached. No background music required.
